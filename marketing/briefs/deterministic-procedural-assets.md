# Brief: deterministic procedural assets

**Reader:** solo game developer evaluating how to add asset variety without
creating unreproducible content.

**Search intent:** learn.

**Promise:** explain why repeatable inputs matter in a production asset pipeline,
without teaching or disclosing a specific generator implementation.

**Required evidence:** `deterministic-generation`.

**Must include:** a practical checklist of inputs a developer should preserve;
the distinction between visual variation and reproducibility.

**Must not include:** implementation details, product comparisons, source code,
or claims about an unreleased product.
