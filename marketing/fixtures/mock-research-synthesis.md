# Research synthesis (fixture)

This fixture is not a recommendation. A live synthesis may only summarize
evidence packets produced by the research director, retain their source URLs,
and recommend `RESEARCH_BRIEF_REVIEW_REQUIRED`, `HOLD_FOR_SEARCH_DATA`, or
`REJECT_OR_MONITOR`.

It may not select a topic for publication, invent demand, use product source,
or produce an article draft.
